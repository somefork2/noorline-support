Help

The times differ from my mosque.
Open Settings → Calculation method and choose the authority your mosque follows (21 are available, including Umm al‑Qura, Diyanet, JAKIM, Kemenag, ISNA, Moonsighting Committee and Jafari). Hanafi Asr is under Settings → Asr. For a minute or two of difference use Fine‑tune.
The Hijri date is one day off.
Local moon sighting can differ from the Umm al‑Qura calendar. Settings → Hijri date adjustment moves it by ±2 days.
Prayer Focus doesn't lock my apps.
Noorline needs Screen Time access: open the Focus tab and allow it, then choose apps. Make sure Prayer Focus is on and the prayer is selected. Locks start at the adhan (plus the delay you chose).
The “I've prayed” button is grey.
It wakes a few minutes after the adhan — about one minute per rak‘ah — so the habit of tapping it at once doesn't sneak in.
I really need my phone during prayer time.
Focus tab → “I need my phone — pause 5 min” (not available in Strict mode). Locks never last longer than the prayer's window.
I don't see the Dynamic Island countdown.
Turn on Settings → Live Activity & Dynamic Island in Noorline and allow Live Activities in iPhone Settings → Noorline. The Dynamic Island exists on iPhone 14 Pro and later; other iPhones show it on the Lock Screen.
No sound at the adhan.
Allow notifications for Noorline and check that a Focus mode isn't silencing them. Choose the sound in Settings → Alerts: spoken reminder, recorded adhan, soft chime or silent.
Why is my streak still alive after travel or my period?
Long‑press a prayer on the Today screen and mark it Excused. Excused prayers keep the streak.
How do I cancel Noorline Pro?
iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Noorline. To restore a purchase: Noorline → Settings → Restore purchases.
Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
