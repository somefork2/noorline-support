Noorline Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Noorline does not collect, store on servers, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

  • Location is used on your iPhone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Prayer log, settings and app choices for Prayer Focus are stored only on your device (in the app's private App Group container).
  • Screen Time: Noorline uses Apple's FamilyControls with your permission to place a shield over the apps you pick during prayer times. Apple gives Noorline only opaque tokens; Noorline cannot see which apps you use or what you do in them.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. Noorline only learns whether Pro is active.

Deleting the app removes all its data. Questions: somefork@gmail.com. Help
